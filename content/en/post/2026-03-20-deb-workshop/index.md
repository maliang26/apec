### 1. Workshop Overview
This workshop aims to provide a systematic introduction to the core principles and applications of Dynamic Energy Budget (DEB) theory. It offers an integrated platform combining theoretical learning with hands-on practice, while fostering the exchange of experiences in applying DEB models across diverse research contexts. The workshop seeks to promote a deeper understanding of DEB theory and encourage its broader and more effective use in scientific research.

### 2. Introduction to DEB Theory
Dynamic Energy Budget (DEB) theory is a metabolic framework that quantitatively describes how organisms acquire, assimilate, and allocate energy and matter throughout their life cycle. Using a unified set of first principles translated into mathematical expressions, DEB theory explains the allocation of resources to maintenance, growth, development, and reproduction. It has been widely applied in ecology, toxicology, aquaculture, conservation biology, climate change research, and related disciplines.

### 3. Speaker Introduction
The theoretical and practical sessions will be delivered by our invited speaker, **Dr. Gonçalo M. Marques**, Researcher and Invited Assistant Professor at the Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon.

Gonçalo M. Marques is a scientist who works at the crossroads of physics, biology, and ecology, and is well known in the DEB community for helping make Dynamic Energy Budget theory practical and usable for real organisms. Trained originally as a physicist, he became fascinated by how living systems manage energy, grow, and interact with their environment. He has been deeply involved in developing tools and methods for estimating DEB parameters and is a member of the board of curators of the Add-my-Pet (AmP) database, which supports DEB studies across thousands of species. Beyond his research, Marques is also an enthusiastic teacher who enjoys explaining complex ideas in clear and intuitive ways, helping students see how DEB links metabolism, life histories, and ecosystems into one coherent framework.
