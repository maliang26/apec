My research focuses on the thermal physiology and adaptive biology of reptiles, with an emphasis on physiological responses and adaptations to extreme heat under climate change. A particular interest is embryonic thermal vulnerability and heat tolerance, including interspecific and population-level variation, the underlying physiological mechanisms, and their genetic basis. My broader research interests include the physiological adaptations of reptiles to changing thermal environments.
